There's two ways to get out of the rat race:

1. Increase your income, well really your passive income, or
2. Decrease your expenses.

Or some combination of those.

Speaking metaphorically for computing, newer systems get more powerful with
time, but it never ends. AI is part of that. I've found agentic coding makes me
far more productive, but it's just part of the same rat race. Maybe I still need
to live in that for my financial life and coping in the world around me.

But sometimes I just want to leave the race. And for computing, that's what this
video is about. For what I actually enjoy doing, this is about building an off
ramp from increasing computer specs, from the internet, and from AI. I'm making
a different corner I can spend some time in.

And as of the very recent past, I'm using AI to help me build that off ramp.

And my project isn't the only game in town, but I'm aiming to build a cheaper
and more standalone system than many others out there. While also still
providing as powerful of a system that I can within the constraints.

Am I Boromir trying to defeat Sauron with the one ring? Am I Luthen using the
enemy's methods to fight them? Well, one difference is that I'm not trying to
destroy anything. Just to make myself a little corner that I'd like to spend
some time in. A place for escape sometimes.
